Ishigaki Optional Tour

Ishigaki Island is located in Okinawa, Japan, and is known for its tropical beaches in the Pacific Ocean.
Enjoy the southern-island atmosphere with the schedule below.

DAY 1

Arrival Day and Sunset by the Sea

  • Arrive at Ishigaki Airport and check in to your hotel
  • Stroll around Kabira Bay and relax on the white-sand beach
  • Visit Ishigaki Limestone Cave in the afternoon (a great option even on rainy days)
  • Enjoy a sunset cruise in the evening
  • Dinner featuring local island ingredients
DAY 2

Ocean Activities Day

  • Morning snorkeling or introductory diving
  • Tropical beachside lunch
  • Afternoon SUP or mangrove kayaking
  • Stargazing tour at night, weather permitting
DAY 3

Island Side Trip and Return

  • Half-day tour to Taketomi or Iriomote Island
  • Walk through red-tile streets and photo spots
  • Buy souvenirs and transfer to the airport
  • Wrap up your trip by sharing your favorite photos
